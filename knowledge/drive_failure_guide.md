# Drive Failure Guide

## Reallocated sectors
A reallocated sector is a bad sector the drive has remapped to a spare area. A few are normal. A steadily rising count means the media is wearing out. Growth that accelerates over days is one of the strongest early failure signals.

## Seek error rate
A rising seek error rate points to mechanical problems such as head positioning faults or vibration. Check for vibration from neighbouring drives and for failing bearings.

## Power-on hours
Drives older than roughly 40,000 power-on hours are in the wear-out part of their life. Age alone is not a failure, but it lowers the margin for other warning signs.

## Combined signals
Two or more of high temperature, rising reallocated sectors, and rising seek errors together mean the drive should be replaced proactively.
