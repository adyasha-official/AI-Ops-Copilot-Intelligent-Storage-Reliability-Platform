# Drive Temperature Guidelines

## Normal operating range
Most enterprise hard drives operate best between 30 and 45 degrees Celsius. Readings in this range need no action.

## Elevated temperature
Sustained temperatures between 45 and 55 degrees Celsius shorten drive life. Inspect cooling conditions: check fans, airflow blockages, dust, and rack placement next to hot neighbours. Review recent telemetry for an increasing trend rather than a single spike.

## Critical temperature
Sustained temperatures above 55 degrees Celsius are critical. Reduce load on the drive, fix cooling immediately, and open an incident. High temperature combined with rising reallocated sectors strongly indicates a deteriorating drive.
