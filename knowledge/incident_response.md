# Incident Response

## Severity levels
HIGH: risk score at or above 0.70. Investigate the same day and plan replacement.
MEDIUM: risk score from 0.40 to 0.69. Investigate this week and monitor the trend daily.
LOW: below 0.40. No action required.

## Workflow
Open an incident when a drive reaches HIGH risk. Move it to IN_PROGRESS when an engineer starts work, and to RESOLVED once the drive is replaced or the cause is fixed. Add the root cause to the description.
