# Maintenance Procedure

## Proactive replacement
1. Confirm the warning signs in the last 7 to 14 days of telemetry.
2. Verify that data on the drive is replicated or backed up.
3. Schedule replacement in the next maintenance window.
4. Rebuild the array and verify the rebuild completes.
5. Record the serial number of the removed drive.

## Cooling check
Inspect fan operation, clean dust filters, verify rack airflow direction, and re-measure temperature after one hour.
